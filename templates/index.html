 Guidelines for great cloning

                                
Follow these tips and your clone will sound natural across languages.

                            

                        

                        

                            
✓
Record in a quiet, small room — carpets and curtains absorb echo.

                            
✓
Use an external microphone (USB / condenser), not a laptop's webcam mic.

                            
✓
Pause between paragraphs. Close windows, switch off AC and fans.

                            
✓
Speak with natural intonation — the clone will end up smoother than the source.

                            
✓
Record 5–10 minutes. Variety of speech = higher quality.

                            
✓
Don't pre-process — we'll handle WAV mono conversion automatically.

                        

                    


                    

                        ⚠️ Accent transfer is limited. By default the clone uses a neutral accent —
                        record in the language you plan to use the voice in.

                        Recording Guide
                    

                    

                        Official Elai instructions for recording footage that produces a clean avatar.
                        Follow this checklist before pressing record — most retakes come from missing one of these items.
                        Source: Avatar Creation Instructions.pdf.
                    

                


                

                    
5–7 minduration

                    
4K UHD3840×2160

                    
30 fpsiOS / Android UHD

                    
4–5 × 2–3 ssilent pauses

                    
Eyes to cameradirect contact

                    
Centeredno body cropping

                


                


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
1  Setup & gear

                                
Get the recording environment right before the actor sits down.

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Duration: 5–7 minutes

                                    
Long enough that we can pick the best segment without re-takes.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Background

                                    
Selfie / Scenario avatars: any background works.
Studio avatars: plain, typically green.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Clean the lens

                                    
Wipe your camera / smartphone lens — smudges are amplified at 4K.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Resolution: 4K @ 30 fps

                                    

                                        iOS: Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K, 30 fps.

                                        Android: Camera → Menu → Settings → Video → Rear camera resolution → UHD 4K.
                                    

                                

                            

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
2  Look

                                
Anything the camera sees ends up baked into the avatar.

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Hair

                                    
Keep hair neat. Avoid stray hairs and large wavy curls — they break background removal.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Clothing

                                    
For studio avatars wear clothing that contrasts with the background.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Reflections

                                    
Minimize reflections on teeth and glasses.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Shadows & lighting

                                    
Use diffused lighting. Avoid hard shadows under the nose or around the neck.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Beard

                                    
Best to avoid a thick, heavy beard — it confuses the mouth tracker.

                                

                            

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
3  Position & movement

                                
How the actor sits, moves and is framed by the lens.

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Head: side to side, not up & down

                                    
Move the head mostly horizontally — twist left/right. Minimize vertical (nod) motion. No sharp jerks.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Mouth

                                    
Open wide enough to see the teeth and articulation, but don't exaggerate — extreme openings cause artefacts.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Hands below the chest

                                    
Keep hands out of the face/mouth region.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Framing

                                    
Center the actor. Minimize space above the head. Don't crop hands or other body parts on the sides.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Eyes on the lens

                                    
Maintain direct eye contact. Don't roll or turn the eyes away — they are reused for avatar creation.

                                

                            

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
4  Speech & facial expressions

                                
Pacing, articulation and the pauses that make the avatar feel alive.

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Narration

                                    
Slow, clear and expressive. The avatar inherits your style — open the mouth wide enough to capture teeth and articulation.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Pronunciation

                                    
Loud, clear and distinct. Enrich lip movements. Avoid puffing, compressing lips or sticking the tongue out.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Silent pauses — most missed step

                                    

                                        Leave 4–5 pauses of 2–3 seconds between sentences. Mouth closed, eyes still on the lens, no head movement.
                                        The validator will fail the clip without them.
                                    

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Avoid

                                    
Yawning, touching your face, bursts of laughter — all corrupt the training data.

                                

                            

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
5  Submit your material

                                
Hand the file off without quality loss.

                            

                        

                        

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Save at full quality

                                    
Export the original file — no re-compression, no screen recording.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Cloud upload

                                    
Create an Avatar name folder in Google Drive, upload the file, copy the public link.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
Email the link

                                    
Send the shared link to avatars@elai.io.

                                

                            

                        

                    

                


                

                    

                        
 Ready to score a take?

                        
Run the automated validator to make sure your clip hits resolution, fps, duration, sharpness, face coverage, pauses and gaze targets before sending it for training.

                    

                    

                        
                        
                    

                

            


            
            

                

                    
 Validate Footage

                    

                        Pre-flight check for raw avatar footage. Runs the full sensor stack —
                        video (resolution · fps · duration · sharpness · face coverage · gaze),
                        head pose (horizontal twist vs vertical nod),
                        framing (top margin · face size),
                        scene (background uniformity · lighting evenness) and
                        audio (loudness · peaks · silence ratio · closed-mouth pauses) —
                        against the V3.6 + PDF recording spec and returns a pass-or-fix report with specific recommendations.
